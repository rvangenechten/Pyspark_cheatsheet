# Outreach templates

One rule: **never send a pitch without clips attached.** The audit *is* the
pitch. Everything below assumes you already cut 3 clips from that specific
prospect's own episode.

---

## 1. Cold email — the audit (primary)

> **Subject:** 3 clips from your [episode topic] episode
>
> Hi [Name],
>
> I cut three vertical clips out of your [episode #/title] episode and put them
> here: [link]
>
> No charge and nothing to sign — I do this for a handful of [industry] shows
> each week. The one at 14:20 where you talk about [specific moment] is the one
> I'd post first; it's self-contained and it opens with a question, which is
> most of the battle.
>
> If they're useful I'll tell you how I'd run the rest of the back catalogue.
> If not, keep them anyway.
>
> [Your name]
> [link to 2-3 previous clips]

Why it works: specific to their episode, gives before it asks, names a real
timestamp (proving you watched), and the ask is optional.

**Do not:** attach a deck, mention AI, say "I hope this email finds you well",
or list your services.

---

## 2. Follow-up (day 4, if no reply)

> Subject: re: 3 clips from your [topic] episode
>
> Hi [Name] — the clips are still at [link] if you didn't get to them.
>
> One thing I noticed: you've got [N] episodes up and no shorts anywhere. That's
> roughly [N×2] clips of material sitting idle. Happy to explain how I'd work
> through it, or happy to leave it — just let me know which.

One follow-up. Then stop. A third email costs you the referral later.

---

## 3. LinkedIn DM (short form)

> Cut 3 clips from your [topic] episode — [link]. No ask, they're yours. The
> [specific moment] one is the strongest. I do this for [industry] shows.

---

## 4. Reply when they say "how much?"

> Two ways people run this:
>
> **Starter — $750/mo:** 8 clips a month, captioned and ready to post.
> **Growth — $1,500/mo:** 20 clips a month, two hook versions on the best four,
> thumbnail frames, and a monthly note on what performed.
>
> Most [industry] shows your size go Growth, because 8 clips isn't enough to
> learn anything from. First batch lands within 48 hours of your next episode,
> and it's month to month — leave whenever.
>
> Want me to start with [next episode]?

Name the price in writing, immediately, without a call if they ask by email.
Making people book a call to hear a number loses half of them.

---

## 5. Reply to "why not just use OpusClip?"

Answer this one honestly — dodging it loses the deal.

> You absolutely can, and for $15/mo it's a fair deal. What you get is ~30
> clips per episode, ranked by a score, and then someone on your team has to
> watch all 30, pick 4, fix the captions, write the copy and post them. That
> "someone" is the part that doesn't happen.
>
> I use automated tooling too — that's why I can do this at this price. What
> you're paying for is that four finished clips show up ready to post every
> week without you thinking about it, and that they're picked by someone who's
> watched 200 episodes in [industry].

---

## 6. Referral ask (after a good first batch)

> Glad these landed. One favour — who else in [industry] is sitting on a pile
> of episodes nobody's clipping? Happy to do the same free audit for them and
> mention you sent me.

---

## 7. Price-increase note (client 4+)

> Heads up: new clients from [month] are at $1,500 for the Growth tier. You're
> staying at your current rate for as long as you're with me — wanted you to
> hear it from me rather than notice it on the site.

Grandfathering early clients costs little and buys years of goodwill.

---

## Targets that keep you honest

| Metric | Healthy | If below |
|---|---|---|
| Audits sent / week | 50 | Nothing else matters; do this first |
| Reply rate | 15-25% | Your clips are weak — back to the tuning loop |
| Reply → call | 40% | Your follow-up is too pushy or too vague |
| Call → close | 30-50% | You're not naming the price on the call |

50 audits → ~10 replies → ~4 calls → ~1-2 clients. That is the whole funnel.
Two weeks of real audit volume is one client.
