Questions people actually ask

Why not just use an AI clipping tool for $15 a month?

You can, and it's a fair deal. It'll give you thirty clips ranked by a score, and then somebody has to watch all thirty, pick four, fix the captions, write the copy and post them. That somebody is the part that never happens. We use automated tooling too — that's why this is priced the way it is. What you're paying for is four finished clips arriving every week, picked by someone who has watched a lot of episodes in your industry.

Do you guarantee views?

No, and be wary of anyone who does. Platform distribution changes monthly and nobody controls it. We guarantee the clips, the turnaround, and that you can leave any month.

Who owns the clips?

You do, outright, from the moment the invoice is paid.

What about guests?

We only clip footage you have the rights to. If a guest hasn't agreed to commercial reuse, tell us and that episode stays off the list.

Send one episode. You get three clips back within two days, plus a short note on what your long-form is leaving on the table. If they're useful we'll talk. If not, keep them.
